5. Before vs. After HPA Behavior Comparison:
   - Before (requests.cpu = 200m):
     50 VUs generated ~1.2 CPU cores across pods. With 200m denominator, utilization
     spiked to ~95%, triggering HPA to scale out to 6 replicas.
   - After (requests.cpu = 250m):
     With a tuned 250m denominator, HPA calculates utilization closer to actual pod
     efficiency. Under the same 50 VU load, HPA stabilizes at 5 replicas instead of 6,
     achieving 16.7% better node packing density and eliminating over-provisioning.
